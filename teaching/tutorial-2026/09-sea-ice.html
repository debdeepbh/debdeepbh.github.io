Citation chasing

The book claims

the temperature rise of 0.5 C in the twentieth century’s due to man-made carbon dioxide emission

Why is it true and not due to retreating ice age that ended 10,000 years ago?

Activity: Citation chasing

Materials: Laptops, access to the Grinnell Library database, JSTOR, Web of Science, or Google Scholar

Statement: The increase in global mean surface temperatures during the 20th century is primarily the result of human-induced emissions of carbon dioxide and not due to retreating ice age that ended 10,000 years ago”

(20 Minutes)

  • Break into groups of 3 (and 4).
  • Your goal is to unravel the statement by finding the specific pieces of evidence that support it.
  • Find scholarly article(s) that supports the statement.
  • Then, look at the references of that article to find the original source of a specific claim.
  • Continue this process until you have a chain of at least 3 - 4 sources that build the argument.
  • Create a dependency map of the references with arrows.
  • (3 mins per group) present your argument chain to the class
